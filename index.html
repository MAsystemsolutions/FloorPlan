Lote Site Planner

Enter your lot, the slope, and what you want to build. It lays out the site, checks setbacks, and shows the distances between structures.

Start from
Lot (meters)

Quick way to measure: stand at the road, look straight ahead to the back of the lot, and estimate how many "you" heights it drops. One person is about 1.6 m.

Setbacks & spacing (meters)

Defaults follow common R-1 values in the National Building Code (5 m front, 2 m sides and rear). Your city or municipality may set different numbers.

Structures

Width runs along the road. Depth runs from the road going in. Parking goes in the front yard. A septic tank is added automatically when there's a toilet.
